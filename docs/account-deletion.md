# Account and Data Deletion for Phone Down

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Phone Down uses an optional Google sign-in only for cloud backup and restore.

We do not create a separate standalone Phone Down username/password account.
If you connected your Google account in Phone Down, you can remove that connection
and delete the associated app data by following the steps below.

## How to delete your Phone Down account connection and associated data

1. Open `Phone Down`.
2. Go to `Settings > Privacy`.
3. Tap `Delete All Local Data`.
4. If you have cloud backup enabled, turn on `Also delete cloud backup`.
5. Confirm the deletion flow.

This process will:

- remove your local focus sessions, interruptions, and settings from the device
- sign you out of the connected Google account in Phone Down
- delete the app's backup from your Google Drive app data folder if you choose the cloud-backup deletion option

## What data may be retained

- Google Play purchase history is managed by Google Play and is subject to Google's retention policies
- If you do not choose `Also delete cloud backup`, the hidden backup in your Google Drive app data folder may remain until you delete it later from the app

## If you only want to remove data without deleting the account connection

Phone Down also lets you remove app data without deleting your Google account itself:

- delete local app data through `Settings > Privacy > Delete All Local Data`
- remove the cloud backup through the in-app backup deletion flow
- sign out from the account screen if you only want to disconnect Google sign-in
