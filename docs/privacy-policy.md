# Privacy Policy for Phone Down

**Last Updated:** May 3, 2026

## Introduction

Phone Down ("we", "our", or "the app") is a focus and productivity application designed to help you stay present by encouraging you to place your phone face down during focus sessions. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Data We Collect

### Data You Provide
- **Google Account Information**: If you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup, we access your display name, email address, and profile photo. This is handled entirely through Google's authentication system.

### Data Generated by the App
- **Focus Session Data**: Information about your focus sessions including duration, start/end times, interruption events, and completion status. This data is stored locally on your device.
- **App Settings**: Your preferences including theme mode, sound/haptic settings, default timer duration, and backup preferences.
- **Billing Information**: If you purchase Phone Down Pro, transaction details are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not store payment information.

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your location.
- We do not collect your contacts or personal files.
- We do not use advertising identifiers.
- We do not track your activity outside the app.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## How We Use Your Data

### Primary Uses
- **App Functionality**: Session data is used to display your focus history, streaks, and insights within the app.
- **Cloud Backup**: If you opt in, your session data and settings are backed up to your personal Google Drive app data folder.
- **Billing**: Purchase verification is handled through Google Play's billing system.

### What We Don't Do
- We do not use your data for advertising.
- We do not profile you for marketing purposes.
- We do not share data with analytics providers.
- We do not sell your data under any circumstances.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local-First Approach
Phone Down operates primarily offline. All your focus session data and settings are stored locally on your device using:
- **Room Database**: Stores focus sessions and interruption events
- **DataStore**: Stores app preferences and settings

### Cloud Backup (Optional)
If you enable cloud backup:
- Data is stored in your personal Google Drive app data folder
- Only the Phone Down app can access this data
- Backup is encrypted in transit using TLS
- You can delete your backup at any time through the app

### Security Measures
- Auth tokens are stored in encrypted preferences
- Network communications use HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
- Certificate pinning for Google API communications
- No plaintext storage of sensitive data

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access Your Data
All your data is accessible within the app through the Insights and Settings screens.

### Delete Your Data
You can delete all local data at any time through Settings > Privacy > Delete All Local Data. This will:
- Remove all focus sessions and interruption events
- Reset app settings to defaults
- Optionally delete your cloud backup (if enabled)
- Sign you out of your Google account (if connected)

### Export Your Data
Phone Down Pro users can export their session history. Export files are saved locally on your device.

### Account Deletion
To fully delete your account:
1. Delete all local data through the app
2. Sign out of Google account
3. Revoke app access through your Google Account settings

## Children's Privacy

Phone Down is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us to have it removed.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Services
We use Google Play Billing for in-app purchases and Google Sign-In for optional cloud backup. These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

### Google Drive
If you enable backup, data is stored in your Google Drive app data folder. Google Drive's privacy and security policies apply.
