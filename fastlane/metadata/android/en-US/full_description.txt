Phone Down is a focus and productivity app built around one simple rule: place your phone face down to start focusing.

<b>How it works</b>
- Start a focus session
- Place your phone face down on a flat surface
- The timer only counts when your phone stays down and stable
- Picking up your phone pauses the session

<b>Core features (free)</b>
- Unlimited focus sessions
- Timer with preset durations (10, 15, 25, 45, 60 minutes)
- Daily focus summary
- Last 7 days insights
- Streak tracking
- Clean session tracking

<b>Pro features</b>
- Unlimited history and advanced analytics
- Focus quality score and trends
- Heatmap visualization
- Best focus hour and day insights
- Cloud backup to Google Drive
- Data export

<b>Privacy-first</b>
- All session data stays on your device
- Optional cloud backup is opt-in and stored in your personal Google Drive
- No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics

Phone Down helps you build an honest focus habit by making the ritual physical. No gamification, no shame—just a simple tool to help you stay present.
